About Dr. Park

Twenty years behind the exam chair.

For more than two decades, Dr. Park has practiced ophthalmology — including 17 years caring for patients in Washington, DC. She recently relocated her practice to Silver Spring Eye to be closer to family, carrying a lighter, post-pandemic schedule and the same commitment to patient trust she built over nearly two decades in DC.

"Like some of you, I've struggled to find glasses that felt truly comfortable, especially with a higher prescription." — Dr. Park, in a letter to her patients

Outside the exam room, Dr. Park is also the founder of Noon Shop Eyewear, a lightweight eyewear brand born from patterns she noticed in her own patients.

Noon Shop Eyewear

Eyewear, Engineered to Fit

A Frame That Finally Fits

In 2023, Dr. Park founded Noon Shop Eyewear after noticing the same problem again and again in her own exam room: patients with a perfect prescription undone by a frame that didn't fit. Noon Shop makes lightweight, Korean-designed frames engineered for a broader range of faces — low bridges, high cheekbones, and everything in between.
