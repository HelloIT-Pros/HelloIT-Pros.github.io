About Dr. Park

Twenty years behind the exam chair.

For more than two decades, Anna Park, MD has practiced ophthalmology — including 17 years caring for patients in Washington, DC. She recently relocated her practice to Silver Spring Eye to be closer to family, carrying a lighter, post-pandemic schedule and the same commitment to patient trust she built over nearly two decades in DC.

Along the way, she ran into the same problem in her own life that her patients had been describing for years.

"Like some of you, I've struggled to find glasses that felt truly comfortable, especially with a higher prescription." — Dr. Park, in a letter to her patients

That struggle — and years of watching patients leave with a perfect prescription undone by a frame that didn't fit — led her, in 2023, to found Noon Shop Eyewear.

From the Exam Room to Noon Shop

Founded on fit.

Across two decades of practice, Dr. Park customized lenses down to the fraction of a diopter — yet patient after patient came back with the same complaint, not about their vision, but about their frames.

The lenses were perfect. The frames, designed around a narrow, average face, were not: they slid down low bridges, pressed on high cheekbones, and pinched wider faces — quietly undoing the precision of the prescription underneath.

"I watched patient after patient leave with a perfect prescription, and a frame that negated half of it by sliding off their nose. So I set out to bring people eyewear that finally fits." Anna Park, MD · Founder, Noon Shop
