Purpose: Nurture new B2B prospects from intake to appointment booking.

Trigger: Prospect enters CRM via Prospect Intake Form (tag: prospect_new).

Duration: ~150 days (10 emails).

Paired email folder: Prospect Journey V2 (10 templates).

GHL Workflow ID: f5e0305d-9330-4737-8b3e-4ac329535e2c

Flow:

The core issue: Shopify order data arrives in GHL in two places:

1. Payments/Orders module — full line-item detail, amounts, SKUs, fulfillment status (POPULATED)
2. Contact custom fields — last_order_date, customer_status, expected_reorder_date (EMPTY for QB-sourced contacts)

However, for the ~95% of contacts that came through QuickBooks (not directly from Shopify orders), the workflow never fires, leaving their custom fields empty.

3. GHL matches contact by email or creates new

6. If repeat order → Customer Status = "Active Customer", enter Active Customer Journey
