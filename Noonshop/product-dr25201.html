The Frame

MADE TO
DISAPPEAR

The DR25201 exists because Dr. Anna Park had one specific problem to solve: a round frame with a low-bridge geometry that would sit flush on a face that standard eyewear had spent decades ignoring. The result is a frame that performs a small engineering miracle — it looks like nothing, weighs almost nothing, and yet holds exactly where it should, all day, without adjustment.

The oval lens is the defining proportion of the 2025 Dormann line — slightly wider through the middle than the previous DR24100, with a 50 mm lens width that suits a wider mid-face without overwhelming it. The silhouette reads as quietly bold: round enough to be soft, geometric enough to be intentional.

Five colourways were chosen with the same deliberateness as the engineering. Warm Gold (C01) references the gold standard of mid-century optical. Matte Black (C02) is the frame for everyone who wants the frame to disappear. Tortoise Brown (C03) adds warmth without nostalgia. Crystal Grey (C04) is the cool, contemporary option. Ink Havana (C05) is the one for people who know exactly what they want.

The Materials

TR90 &
BETA-Ti

TR90 is an aerospace-grade thermoplastic elastomer. It was developed for applications where failure under stress is not acceptable — eyewear being one of them. It's lighter than acetate, lighter than standard plastic, and structurally rigid in a way that neither material can match at this weight. At the nose contact zones, the TR90 is skin-safe: no nickel, no coating required.

The temples are beta-titanium — a shape-memory alloy that returns to its preset geometry after flexing up to 40° without fatigue. This matters specifically for people with wider cheekbones: a standard spring-hinge frame pushes back against the face. Beta-titanium temples simply conform and hold. They're also pre-angled for a lower head profile — meaning the frame doesn't ride high or sit tilted, a constant issue with frames calibrated for European anatomy.

The hinge is stainless steel with nickel content — but positioned specifically so that no nickel component contacts skin. The multi-layer UV lacquer coat is applied dry, not dipped, for even coverage without added weight.
