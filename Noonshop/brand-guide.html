Brand Voice

HOW WE
SPEAK.

DIRECT

We say what we mean. No filler, no fluff. Our copy earns every word.

"The right frame. Properly fitted. Guaranteed."

CONFIDENT

We believe in what we make. The brand speaks with quiet assurance, not loudness.

"Noon Shop. For people who want to see clearly."

HUMAN

Behind the precision is warmth. We're solving a real problem for real people.

"Born from the frustration of frames that never quite fit."

REFINED

We draw on craft, not trends. Language that ages well. Minimalist, considered phrasing.

"Optical precision, without the prescription attitude."

Do
  • Use Bebas Neue for all display type
  • Pair headlines with Playfair italic subtitles
  • Default to dark backgrounds (#0C0C0C)
  • Keep spacing generous and deliberate
  • Use photography that shows material and detail
  • Let whitespace do the talking
Don't
  • Use decorative or script typefaces
  • Add color accents beyond the core palette
  • Crowd layouts with multiple competing elements
  • Use bold weight in Outfit for body copy
  • Place the logo on busy or patterned backgrounds
  • Use exclamation marks in brand copy
Photography

IMAGE
DIRECTION.

Product photography leads with material and craftsmanship. Frames on white or paper. Macro detail. No props, no lifestyle clutter — let the product carry the frame.

Preferred
Frames on pure white or paper background. Material visible. Frame fills 60–80% of frame.
Acceptable
Detail macro shots showing hinge, temple, nose pad. High contrast. Studio lit.
Avoid
Lifestyle photography with people, outdoor settings, props, or anything that competes with the product.
Motion & Interaction

HOW WE
MOVE.

ENTER

Elements fade in from below (Y+24–28px). Duration: 0.7–0.8s. Ease: power3.out. Stagger 100ms between children.

HOVER

Cards lift translateY(-4px). Links underline scales from right. Arrows extend width. Duration: 0.3–0.45s. Ease: cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1).

EXIT

Fade up and out (Y-12px). Duration: 0.4–0.6s. Ease: power2.in. Used in loader and transition states.

Movement is calm and intentional. Never rapid, never decorative. Every animation earns its place.
