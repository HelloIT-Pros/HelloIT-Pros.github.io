Key Finding

The “Data Island” Problem

Shopify order data arrives in GHL in two disconnected places:

  • Payments module: Full line-item detail, amounts, SKUs ✓
  • Contact custom fields: Empty for ~95% of contacts ✗

The bridge (Order Monitor workflow) only fires on live Shopify events — it never ran for the 17K+ contacts imported via QuickBooks.
