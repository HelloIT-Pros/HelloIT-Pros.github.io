Phase 1  ·  Testing & Calibration
Important Rule — Testing Phase

For the next week, we are strictly operating in a testing environment. This is a hard rule during testing:

Only test contacts should be used
Only test email accounts should be used
No campaigns or automations assigned to real prospects
Purpose of Testing Phase

What We Are Validating

  • Validate the automations are triggering correctly
  • Test all workflows end-to-end
  • Ensure emails deliver correctly to inboxes
  • Make adjustments before going live
Setup Required

Test Email Accounts

Everyone involved in testing must add a test email account to their devices. You can use the test Gmail we created or create your own. Add it to:

  • Your phone
  • Outlook
  • Wherever you check your email

This allows you to receive campaign emails and properly test the workflows.

Sales Process Forms
Form 1  ·  Sales Reps

New Prospect Form

Whenever a salesperson meets a new prospect, they should enter them using this form. We recommend adding it to your phone for quick access.

  • Every prospect enters the CRM correctly
  • The appropriate communication journey begins automatically
Form 2  ·  Sales Operations Only

New Order Entry Form

A manual step used by Sales Operations (not Sales Reps) to record completed orders. This allows the CRM to:

  • Update the customer's journey stage
  • Record the last order date
  • Calculate the expected reorder date
  • Automatically send reorder emails at the right time

Will be demonstrated during today's walkthrough.

Setup Actions for the Team
Calendar

Connect Outlook Calendars

Each salesperson must connect their Outlook calendar to Go High Level. This enables the booking system to work correctly and prevents double-booking.

  • Required for appointment booking pages to function
  • Ensures real-time calendar availability
Mobile App

Download the GHL CRM App

Download the Go High Level mobile app to manage contacts, view pipelines, and receive notifications on the go.

  • Available on iOS and Android
  • Log in with your GHL credentials
Quick Access

Add GHL to Your Home Screen

Add the Go High Level browser page to your phone or device home screen for fast one-tap access without opening a browser each time.

  • Open GHL in Safari or Chrome
  • Tap Share → "Add to Home Screen"
  • Access your CRM instantly from your home screen
All Sales Reps

New Prospect Form

Every sales rep should save quick access to the New Prospect Form so they can add contacts on the spot — in the field, at a meeting, or on the go.

  • Add the form to your phone home screen
  • Save it to your browser favorites
  • The faster prospects are entered, the sooner the CRM journey begins
Sales Operations Only

New Order Entry Form

Sales Operations staff should save the New Order Entry Form for quick access when recording completed orders.

  • Add the form to your phone home screen
  • Save it to your browser favorites
  • Timely order entry ensures reorder campaigns trigger at the right time
