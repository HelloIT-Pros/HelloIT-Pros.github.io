Data Sync & Integration Notes

The following outlines how data flows between GoHighLevel (GHL) and QuickBooks (QB). Understanding these rules is important to avoid data inconsistencies across systems.

Action Syncs to Other System? Note
New customer created in GHL Does Not Sync Will not be created in QuickBooks. QB records must be managed separately.
Customer name updated in QB Syncs to GHL Name change in QuickBooks will update the corresponding contact in GHL.
Customer phone updated in QB Syncs to GHL Phone number change in QuickBooks will update the corresponding contact in GHL.
Customer phone updated in GHL Does Not Sync Phone number changes made in GHL will not carry over to QuickBooks. Update QB separately.
Field Mappings & Flows

The following outlines how specific fields are mapped between systems and how they flow through the CRM.

Source Field Maps To Note
QB  →  Company Name GHL  →  Business Name QuickBooks Company Name syncs directly into the Business Name field in GoHighLevel.
Prospect Form  →  Company Name GHL  →  Business Name When a salesperson submits the New Prospect Form, the Company Name field maps to Business Name in GHL.
GHL  →  Business Name Email Campaigns  →  Practice Name Email campaigns reference the Business Name field to personalize the body of emails with the customer's practice name.
