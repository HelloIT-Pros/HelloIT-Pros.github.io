The Frame

GERMAN
STEEL,
KOREAN FIT

The SB82514 begins with a contradiction: a frame this thin has no right to be this strong. Medical-grade German stainless steel — the same material classification used in surgical instruments and implant hardware — is drawn to a wall thickness that most optical manufacturers don't attempt. The result is a rim that reads as almost two-dimensional from the front, but holds its geometry under daily wear without deformation.

The panto silhouette — that classic rounded lower lens, slightly flatter top — was chosen by Wolfgang Proksch specifically for its ability to complement a wider mid-face. The rounded lower edge softens the jawline optically. The flatter crown creates a brow line without competing with the forehead. It's a shape that has a century of optical history behind it, redrawn for a face geometry it was never originally calibrated for.

Three colourways: Gunmetal Grey (GY) — the steel colour, barely treated, letting the material speak. Matte Black (MB) — the stealth option, disappears against almost every complexion. Matt Metal (MM) — a raw, uncoated steel finish that develops a subtle patina with wear.

The Fit Engineering

49 MM.
21 MM.
145 MM.

Those three numbers — lens width, bridge, temple length — are a fit statement. The 49 mm lens width is narrower than the Dormann oval, making it the choice for someone who wants a more graphic, structured look without the frame overshooting the face width. The 21 mm bridge is the lowest in the Steel Brown line — set for a nose bridge that sits at or below the standard European reference point.

The temple length of 145 mm is calibrated for a shorter ear-to-bridge distance — a common proportion in the faces Noon Shop was built for. A 150 mm temple on a shorter face creates pressure just behind the ear, even if the front fit looks perfect. 145 mm closes that gap precisely.

There are no spring hinges. The stainless frame is set and stays set. This is deliberate: spring hinges accommodate misfit — they allow a too-wide temple to flex to a wider face, masking a fit problem rather than solving it. The SB82514 doesn't need to accommodate. It fits correctly from the first wear.
