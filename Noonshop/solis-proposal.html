Platform Capabilities

Six Marketing Capabilities.
One platform.

SOLIS covers the full spectrum of Noon Shop's content needs, from product page copy to campaign photography to clinical fit notes. Everything generated on-brand, every time.

01 · Intelligence
AI Product Intelligence

Structured product intelligence and merchandising data: names, descriptions, positioning copy, and fit classifications for every Noon Shop frame.

  • Product names & subtitles
  • Ecommerce descriptions
  • Fit-focused positioning copy
  • Frame shape & fit classification
  • Collection & campaign storytelling
02 · Photography
AI Photography Generation

Five distinct photography types, all on-brand. Product flats, macro detail, AI model portraits, editorial campaign imagery, and collection photography.

  • Product flat & ecommerce imagery
  • Macro: hinges, materials, textures
  • AI model: multiple demographics
  • Editorial & campaign imagery
  • Portrait angles: front, 3/4, profile
03 · Marketing
Marketing Content Generation

Social media creative, email campaigns, collection launch content, and product storytelling, all aligned with Noon Shop's brand voice and editorial style.

  • Social media creative & captions
  • Email campaigns: subject, copy, CTA
  • Collection launch content
  • Fit-focused educational content
  • Campaign concepts & storytelling
04 · Ecommerce
Ecommerce Enrichment

Complete product data enrichment for your Shopify storefront: specifications, clinical notes, fit classifications, and review enhancement workflows.

  • Product specifications
  • Fit profile classifications
  • PDP copy & SEO content
  • Fit-focused merchandising
  • Review enhancement workflows
05 · Clinical
Clinical Product Notes

AI-generated "Dr. Anna Park Clinical Notes" for individual frames, reinforcing clinical credibility, fit education, and product differentiation across Noon Shop's catalogue.

  • Cheekbone clearance commentary
  • Low bridge fit notes
  • Optical alignment positioning
  • Comfort & fit geometry
  • Human approval workflow required
06 · Reviews
Review Enhancement

Transform real customer and staff feedback into structured, fit-forward reviews. Authenticity preserved and enriched with fit profile indicators, face classifications, and comfort commentary.

  • Staff & sample user input
  • Fit profile & face-type tagging
  • Structured comfort commentary
  • Demographic & location formatting
  • Moderation workflow required
