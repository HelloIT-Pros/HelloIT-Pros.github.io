How It Works

SKU in.
Content out.

Solis connects your live Shopify SKU data with your brand guide and turns them into publish-ready content — names, descriptions, and photography briefs — without manual copywriting.

Every output follows Noon Shop's exact voice rules. Clinical-warm. Spec-accurate. Consistent across every collection, every launch.

01
Select a Frame
Pick any SKU from the live Shopify-synced database. All measurements, material, and collection data are pre-loaded — nothing to type.
02
Configure Output
Choose what to generate: product names, description, or a full photography brief. Set the model, shot type, and setting for photo direction.
03
Generate in Seconds
Solis reads the brand guide and frame data and outputs on-brand content instantly. Three name options, one definitive description, a three-shot photography brief.
04
Review & Publish
Submit for review. A Reviewer approves the content. It pushes directly to Shopify as a draft — ready to go live.

Platform Features

Built for
Noon Shop.

Every feature in Solis is purpose-built for Noon Shop's catalogue, brand structure, and Shopify workflow. Nothing generic. Nothing to configure from scratch.

01 · Generate
Content Generation

Product names (3 options per run), descriptions (spec-accurate, 65 words max), and photography briefs — all in Noon Shop's clinical-warm voice. Generated from real frame data, not guesswork.

Names · Description · Photo Brief
02 · Data
Shopify + Spec Sync

Every SKU pulled live from Shopify. Manufacturer spec sheets uploaded and parsed into structured frame data — bridge, lens, temple, material, weight. All pre-loaded at generation time.

Live · Always Current
03 · Brand
Living Brand Guide

An editable brand document that feeds every generation. Voice rules, model assignments, collection notes — updated by Admins and read fresh on each run. The brand guide is the source of truth.

Editable · Live Context
04 · Photography
Photography Direction

Three-shot briefs per SKU: primary model shot, second angle, flat-lay. Choose the model persona, shot type, and setting. Each brief includes full creative direction ready for the photographer.

3 Shots Per SKU
05 · Review
Approval Workflow

Role-based access across Owner, Admin, Reviewer, and Viewer. Generated content enters a review queue before publishing. Approved content pushes to Shopify as a draft with a full audit trail.

Shopify Draft on Approval
06 · Assets
Asset Library

All generated content — copy, briefs, photography — organised by collection, type, and status. A campaign builder groups assets into packages ready for channel delivery.

Organised · Campaign-Ready
