The Brief

A site built
around the fit.

Most eyewear brands sell frames. Noon Shop sells the certainty that the frame will fit. The website has to do the same: every page earns trust, educates on fit, and removes the hesitation that comes from buying eyewear online without trying it on.

What Fitting Actually Means

Three dimensions.
One perfect fit.

Most brands size by frame width and stop there. Noon Shop engineers around three clinical dimensions, the same ones Dr. Park identified through two decades of watching prescriptions underperform because the frame didn't fit.

01
Bridge Fit

The nose bridge is where most frames fail. A bridge that's too wide slides down, shifts the optical centre, and defeats the prescription. Noon Shop's adjustable bridge system addresses the most common misfit in eyewear.

"The bridge is doing two jobs: supporting the frame and maintaining optical alignment. Most frames only do one of them."

Dr. Anna Park, MD
02
Cheekbone Clearance

Frames that sit on the cheekbones shift with every expression, smudge the lens, and cause constant unconscious adjustment. Rim height is an engineering choice, and Noon Shop makes it deliberately.

"I had patients coming in for adjustments every few weeks. The same frames, the same problem. The rim was sitting on the cheekbone."

Dr. Anna Park, MD
03
Face Width

Temple length determines whether a frame stays put or creates pressure points. Too wide and the frame falls forward. Too narrow and it grips. Noon Shop proportions temple length to actual face width, not a one-size approximation.

"Fit isn't a detail. It's the whole product. Every other quality decision is irrelevant if the frame doesn't stay where it's supposed to."

Dr. Anna Park, MD

The Founder
DR. ANNA

Ophthalmologist · Founder

"I watched patient after patient leave my clinic with a perfect prescription, and a frame that negated half of it by sliding off their nose."

Dr. Anna Park, MD · Founder

After two decades in ophthalmology, Dr. Park stopped adjusting patients' frames and started designing better ones. Noon Shop is the result: two collections, three fit dimensions, and a single clinical conviction that the frame is as important as the prescription.

Dr. Park built Noon Shop because no one else was solving the right problem. Not frame style. Not lens quality. Not price. Fit. The single variable that determines whether a perfect prescription actually works, and that the eyewear industry had treated as an afterthought for decades.
