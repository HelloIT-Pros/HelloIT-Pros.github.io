Our Story

Founded
on fit.

Noon Shop began in an ophthalmologist's exam room — with a problem the eyewear industry had ignored for decades. This is the story of how a doctor decided to fix it.

The Observation

Twenty years.
One pattern.

Dr. Anna Park spent more than two decades as a board-certified ophthalmologist, customizing lenses down to the fraction of a diopter. Yet patient after patient returned with the same complaint — not about their vision, but about their frames.

The lenses were perfect. The frames were not. Designed around a narrow, average face, they slid down low bridges, pressed on high cheekbones, and pinched wider faces — quietly undoing the precision of the prescription underneath.

She realized the industry had solved the wrong half of the problem. Fit, the variable that determines whether a perfect prescription actually works, had been treated as an afterthought.

"I watched patient after patient leave with a perfect prescription — and a frame that negated half of it by sliding off their nose. So I stopped adjusting frames, and started designing better ones."

Dr. Anna Park, MD · Founder, Noon Shop

The Founder
DR. ANNA
PARK, MD
Board-Certified Ophthalmologist · Founder

With more than twenty years of clinical experience, Dr. Park built Noon Shop to close a gap she saw every day: while lenses are customized to each prescription, frames were designed around a limited, average set of facial standards that never reflected the diversity of real patients.

Noon Shop curates and develops eyewear engineered for a broader range of features — low-bridge noses, higher cheekbones, and diverse face shapes — combining medical insight with innovative design to deliver comfort, visual performance, and lightweight style.
