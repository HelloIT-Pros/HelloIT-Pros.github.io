07 The image gate Live now

Held for review

Before anything is kept

Nothing is kept unchecked

These are the six things this kind of image gets wrong about eyewear. A person confirms each one, by name, before the file exists anywhere.

  • Face matches the model’s anchor. Same heritage reading, same hair, same structure.
  • Shape, bridge and material match the product photograph.
  • Lens, bridge and front width in proportion to the product photograph.
  • Eyes fully in focus. No refraction blur through the lenses.
  • Visible pore detail. Not plastic, not AI–smooth.
  • Setting, light, lens, palette and crop match the chosen scene.
Discard Keep Six of six · kept

The record gate

Approved, or named and held Never sent wrong and never dropped quietly.

Approved content queues for Shopify. Before it goes, the record is checked against what the storefront needs. A style that would publish something wrong is held, and the gate says which field.

What is sent is frozen when it is queued, so it can be diffed against the live store and rolled back by re–pushing an earlier payload.
