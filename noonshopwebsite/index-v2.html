Our Founder

"I watched patient after patient leave with a perfect prescription, and a frame that negated half of it by sliding off their nose."

Anna Park, MD · Ophthalmologist · Founder

After more than twenty years in ophthalmology, Anna Park set out to change how eyewear fits, curating and developing frames engineered for a broader range of faces, in lightweight medical-grade materials. More than 80,000 frames later, Noon Shop is headquartered in Silver Spring, Maryland, still built on a single conviction: the frame is as important as the prescription.
