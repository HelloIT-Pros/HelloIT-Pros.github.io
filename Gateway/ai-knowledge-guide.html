How to share documents with your AI knowledge base

The Gateway AI Assistant can answer questions directly from your company's documents — contracts, policies, handbooks, and more. To keep it accurate and current, HelloIT Pros manages what gets added to the system. This guide explains what to send us, how to send it, and what to keep out.

What You Can Share

Any document that staff regularly reference or need to search through is a good candidate. Contracts are the primary use case — the AI is built specifically to let you search, compare, and ask questions across all of your agreements without hunting through file folders.

Contracts & Agreements — the core use case

  • Vendor service contracts (landscaping, snow removal, pest control, elevators, fire monitoring…)
  • Renewal quotes & proposals
  • Amendments or addenda to existing contracts
  • Lease templates (blank/standard form — see note on executed leases below)
  • Management agreements
  • Insurance certificates & coverage summaries
  • Utility & service agreements

Policies & Reference Documents

  • Employee handbook & HR policies
  • Policy & Procedures manual
  • Site manager reference guides
  • Property-specific operating procedures
  • Approved compliance checklists
  • Vendor contact sheets (text-based)

Do not share

  • Executed tenant leases (contain SSNs, income data — use the blank template instead)
  • Tenant applications or screening reports (contains PII)
  • Bank statements or payment records (financial data)
  • HUD / LIHTC inspection reports with unit-level data (protected)
  • Password lists or login credentials (security risk)
  • Any document still in draft (wait for final)
  • Personal correspondence or emails

File Formats

The format of a document affects how well the AI can read it. Use the best format available.

Works well

  • Word documents (.docx)
  • Digitally-created PDFs (exported from Word or Google Docs)
  • Plain text files (.txt)
  • Scanned PDFs (readable scans — we handle OCR)

Avoid or convert first

  • Excel spreadsheets (.xlsx) — export to text list instead
  • Photos of documents (.jpg, .png) — send the original file
  • Password-protected PDFs — remove protection first
  • Very low-quality scans — text won't extract reliably

Note on scanned documents: If you only have a scanned PDF (like a signed contract), send it — we can work with it. Just know that poor-quality or handwritten scans may need cleanup before they're fully searchable.

How to Name Your Files

The filename becomes part of how the AI cites its sources. A clear name helps both us and the AI know exactly what a document is.

Suggested pattern: Vendor_Property_Type_Year.pdf

How to Send Documents to Us

You have two options — use whichever is most convenient.

A

Shared Google Drive folder

Drop files into the Gateway AI — Document Submissions shared folder in Google Drive. HelloIT Pros monitors this folder and will process new files within 1–2 business days. This is the preferred method for batches of documents.

B

Email to helpdesk

Send an email to helloit@gwaffordable.com with the document attached. Include a brief note: what it is, which property it applies to (if applicable), and whether it replaces an existing document in the system.

What to Include When You Send a Document

A quick note with the document saves time and prevents mistakes. Include:

1

What the document is

e.g., "This is the renewed pest control contract for Spring Manor, covering 2025–2026."

2

Which property (if applicable)

e.g., "Langston Lane only" or "All properties." Helps us add it to the right collection.

3

Whether it replaces something

e.g., "This replaces the 2024 version already in the system." We'll remove the old one so the AI doesn't give conflicting answers.

4

Any expiry or effective date

e.g., "Contract runs through October 2026." We'll schedule a reminder to remove or update it when it expires.

Privacy & What Stays Out

The AI Assistant is designed for operational documents — things your staff needs to look up when doing their jobs. Certain categories of information must never be added:

⚠ Never share these with us for AI ingestion

  • Tenant personal information — names, Social Security numbers, income verifications, lease applications, background checks
  • Financial account data — bank account numbers, routing numbers, payment card details
  • HUD or LIHTC reports that include individual unit or household data
  • Login credentials or passwords of any kind
  • Anything marked confidential that only specific individuals should see

If you're not sure whether a document is appropriate to share, email us first and we'll advise.

Keeping the Knowledge Base Current

The AI is only as accurate as the documents in it. Here are a few situations where you should notify us:

Contract renews

Send us the new contract. We'll replace the old one so the AI reflects the current terms, pricing, and expiry.

Contract expires

Let us know if a vendor relationship ends. We'll remove the document so the AI doesn't quote terms from a contract that no longer applies.

Policy changes

If a section of the Employee Handbook or P&P manual is updated, send the revised document (or just the revised section) and indicate what changed.

New property

When a property is added to the portfolio, include any property-specific vendor contracts or operating procedures in your onboarding handoff.

Property closes

Notify us so we can remove or archive that property's documents. Stale data produces wrong answers.

What to Expect After You Send a Document

1

We receive and review

HelloIT Pros checks the document for format, quality, and content suitability. We'll reach out if anything needs to be adjusted.

2

We prepare and upload

We rename the file if needed, remove the old version if applicable, and upload it to the appropriate collection in the AI Assistant.

3

We verify it's working

We run a quick test query to confirm the AI can find and cite information from the new document accurately.

4

It's live

Staff can query the document in the AI Assistant. If you run a test and something doesn't look right, let us know and we'll investigate.
