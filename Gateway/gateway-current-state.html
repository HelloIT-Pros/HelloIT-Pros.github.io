Prompt Library

Slash-command prompts available to all users. Invoked by typing the command in any chat.

/contract-summary Attach a vendor contract and receive a structured summary covering parties, scope, pricing, term dates, renewal conditions, and termination clauses.
/tenant-notice Draft a formal notice letter from Gateway Management to a tenant. Accepts inputs for tenant name, property, unit, and subject matter.
