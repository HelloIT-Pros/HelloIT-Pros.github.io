Gateway Performance Platform

Scope of Work

Prepared by HelloIT Pros for Gateway Management Services. Based on the July 10, 2026 review with Mike and follow-up clarifications.

1. Overview

Stand up the Gateway Performance Platform as a hosted, login-gated application with two views (Owner and Regional), user management, filters, three years of history, and a data pipeline fed by property reports emailed to the system parser. This document lists the platform requirements plus, for each view, what to show, how much detail, where the data is best sourced, and what should not be shown.

2. Views and Access Model

The platform has two views. Users are assigned to a view based on the properties they are allowed to see.

ViewWho sees itWhat it covers
Owner ViewProperty ownersAn owner sees only the property or properties they own. One owner can be assigned multiple properties if applicable. Owners never see another owner's property.
Regional ViewExecutives and management (Gateway staff)A regional roll-up across multiple properties. Staff see only the properties assigned to them, not necessarily the whole portfolio.

2.1 User management (to be built)

  • Create and manage user accounts (owners, executives, management staff).
  • Assign each user to a view and to one or more specific properties.
  • Enforce access so users only see data for their assigned properties.
  • Login / authentication for all users.

3. Filters and History

  • Three years of history. The platform retains three years of data for every property.
  • Timeframe selector. Users pick the time window within the three-year history (for example a specific month, quarter, or year).
  • Property selector. Users pick one or multiple properties they are allowed to see. Owners default to their own property; management can multi-select across their region.
  • Occupancy comparisons. With three years stored, occupancy can be compared vs. the 3-month average, vs. the same time last year, and across the multi-year trend.

4. Data Ingestion (to be built)

  • Reports come from RealPage / OneSite, either pulled manually or sent automatically by a scheduled email scheduler, and are emailed to the platform.
  • Build an email intake that receives those reports and hands them to the system parser.
  • The parser reads each report, maps it to the data model, and stores it against the correct property and period.
  • Note: OneSite data is near real-time and reports can be scheduled to run and send daily, so the same intake supports a daily cadence later.

5.2 Do not show (Owner)

  • Income Breakdown donut (rent vs. assistance vs. misc): irrelevant and a duplicate of Total Income; in affordable housing owners do not track the split.
  • Variance Analysis (Actual vs. Budget): on hold; opens a can of worms with owners (for example depreciation). Revisit later, tailored.
  • Security deposits tile: irrelevant to owners.
  • Net profit / accumulated profit / custom financials: owners do this themselves from the monthly report package.

6.3 Do not show / de-prioritize (Regional)

  • Tax and Insurance Reserve, Open Payables, other GL accounts: accounting items, not operational; keep them off the top row.
  • Open Work Orders: no source report identified in the current data; omit for v1 unless a work-order report is provided.
  • Miscellaneous income (MTD): low operational value; omit unless specifically wanted.
