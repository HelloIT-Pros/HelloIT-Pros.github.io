Share test

Proving a generated PDF reaches Outlook from this device before anything is built on top of it.

Environment

Run this twice: once in Safari, then again from the icon after Add to Home Screen. The installed case is the one in doubt.

The tests

1 and 2 differ only in whether the PDF exists before the tap. iOS can drop the user gesture across an await, so if 1 works and 2 does not, the real feature has to build the file before the Send button is live. 3 separates "files cannot be shared" from "PDFs cannot be shared". 4 confirms the PDF is valid when nothing is shared at all.

Show the PDF, then use the iOS share button

These test the other route: display the PDF and let iOS provide the share button, rather than calling share from the app. The thing that can fail here is the display, not the sharing. Safari has a long history of rendering a generated PDF as a blank page, and an installed app may refuse to open a new tab at all. For each of these the log only records what the browser reported. You have to tell me what you actually saw: the letter, a blank page, or nothing happening. 7 leaves the app, so use the app switcher to come back.
